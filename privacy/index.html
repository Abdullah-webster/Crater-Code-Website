Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: October 1, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

At CRATER CODE ("we", "our", or "us"), privacy and data sovereignty are fundamental architectural tenets. This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, and protection of personal data across our digital platforms, software consulting engagements, and Crater Code Academy educational programs.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide high-caliber engineering services and educational experiences:

  • Client Inquiries: Name, work email address, company name, project specifications, and technical requirements submitted via our consultation forms.
  • Academy Applicants: Full name, contact details, academic/professional background, and program preferences provided during bootcamp application and enrollment.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized usage metrics, browser diagnostics, and device types to optimize WebGL and high-performance frontend delivery.

3. Confidentiality & Intellectual Property

All client proprietary codebases, proprietary algorithms, database schemas, and commercial strategies shared under Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) are treated with maximum confidentiality and stored in isolated, encrypted cloud environments.

4. Data Protection & Security

We deploy defense-in-depth security standards including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, strict least-privilege IAM controls, and continuous automated dependency scanning.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes strictly essential session tokens and performance telemetry. We do not sell personal data or utilize intrusive third-party cross-site advertising trackers.

6. Contact Data Protection Officer

For inquiries regarding data protection, right-to-be-forgotten requests, or compliance inquiries, please contact our Data Governance team at info@cratercode.com.
